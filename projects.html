About the Project

Spice Garden is a restaurant website concept created to provide visitors with an attractive online experience. The website introduces the restaurant, displays its dining atmosphere and highlights food collections.

The design focuses on simple navigation, attractive food photography, readable content and a responsive layout that works across desktop and mobile devices.

What This Website Offers

Restaurant Introduction

Presents the restaurant, its atmosphere and dining experience.

Food Gallery

Displays food collections and restaurant interior photographs.

Recipe Collection

Highlights popular dishes through an attractive recipe gallery.

Contact Information

Helps visitors connect with the restaurant for enquiries and reservations.

Restaurant Gallery

A visual look at the restaurant environment, food presentation and dining experience.

Freshly Prepared with Care

The website also highlights the food preparation experience and the effort behind every dish.

Through food photography and carefully organized content, visitors can explore the restaurant's culinary experience.
